## A. Method and scoping

1. **The prompt is one sentence. What do you do first?** Ask scope questions for about five minutes: users, frequency, latency feel, freshness, cost of an error, budget. Proof: [playbook §3](interview-playbook.md#3-five-questions-that-each-drive-a-decision).
2. **Why not start with the architecture diagram?** Without numbers every box is a guess, and you cannot defend it under follow-ups. Proof: [playbook §1](interview-playbook.md#1-what-the-interviewer-is-actually-scoring).
3. **How do you split 45 minutes? And 60?** 5/5/15/15/5; with 60, the extra goes to design and deep dive. Proof: [playbook §2](interview-playbook.md#2-the-45-minute-budget).
4. **Functional vs non-functional requirements here?** Functional: what users can do (chat, feedback, uploads, hand-off). Non-functional: latency, availability, cost, residency, audit. Proof: [case 1](interview-cases/01-bank-assistant.md).
5. **What is the user flow vs the data flow?** User flow is the screens and actions; data flow is which services and stores touch the request, which is where residency rules bite. Proof: [case 1](interview-cases/01-bank-assistant.md#high-level-design).
6. **What separates a senior answer?** Asking what a wrong answer costs and deriving each decision from a number. Proof: junior vs senior table in [playbook §1](interview-playbook.md).

## B. Estimation

7. **Turn daily requests into QPS.** Divide by 86,400 seconds; multiply by a justified peak factor (2-5x). Proof: `test_talk_10m_traffic` in `tests/test_interview_envelope.py`.
8. **Why split input and output tokens?** Output is priced higher and is generated one token at a time, so one blended price misstates cost. Proof: `cost_per_request` in `sysdesign/ext/envelope.py`.
9. **Daily cost is under the daily figure. Within budget?** Not if the target is per conversation: divide daily cost by conversations. In the table above it fails. Proof: `test_planted_mistake_daily_passes_conversation_fails`.
10. **How do you choose a routing mix?** Search the mix grid for the highest-quality mix whose cost per conversation fits, with a floor for hard cases on the top tier. Proof: `best_mix`, `test_best_mix_meets_target_and_beats_cheapest_on_quality`.
11. **How many agent steps can you afford?** Budget divided by worst-case step cost, rounded down. Proof: `test_agent_steps_bank_case`, `test_step_cap_from_budget`.
12. **What if no mix fits?** Change the inputs: shorter context, caching, a cheaper tier, or renegotiate the target. Proof: `test_best_mix_none_when_impossible`.
13. **What do you get wrong by planning from requests only?** Request count stays flat while tokens per request grow; cost and latency follow tokens. Proof: [Jagadish-llm-capacity-planning](https://github.com/jagadishmazure-jpg/Jagadish-llm-capacity-planning), [cost-model-10m.md](cost-model-10m.md).
14. **How would you sanity-check the calculator itself?** Hand-check one example and reject bad inputs (negative, NaN, mix not summing to one). Proof: `test_rejects_negative_and_nan_inputs`, `test_mix_must_sum_to_one`.

## C. Routing and cost

15. **Why three tiers rather than one big model?** Most traffic is simple; sending it all to the frontier tier multiplies spend (see the all-frontier row above). Proof: `test_router_ladder_cuts_cost`.
16. **How does the router decide?** Rules, then a classifier, then small, then larger tiers; human-only intents never reach a model. Proof: `test_hitl_intents_never_reach_ai`.
17. **What does cutting cost do to quality and safety?** More small-tier traffic means more misses on hard cases; the golden set is the guard. Proof: [playbook limits](interview-playbook.md#limits).
18. **How do you stop one tenant burning the budget?** Per-user token budgets and a share of the model-call cap. Proof: `test_token_budget_exhaustion_429`, `test_one_user_cannot_take_every_model_slot`.

## D. Latency

19. **How fast must it feel, and what follows?** If first-token time matters, stream and keep a smaller model on the hot path. Proof: `test_sse_stream_tokens_and_ttft_metric`.
20. **Why is p99 bad when the median is fine?** Fan-out multiplies the chance of one slow dependency. Proof: `test_hedging_cuts_tail`, [tail-latency.md](tail-latency.md).
21. **Would you hedge a transfer request?** No: only idempotent reads are hedged. Proof: `test_writes_never_hedged`.
22. **When do you keep the LLM off the hot path?** When the decision deadline is tens of milliseconds. Proof: [case 7](interview-cases/07-low-latency.md).

## E. Knowledge, RAG and fine-tuning

23. **Why not fine-tune on the policies?** Facts change weekly; retrieval with reindexing keeps them current. Fine-tune for style and behaviour. Proof: `test_cdc_reindexes_only_changed_chunk`.
24. **A policy changed. How does the cache know?** Change-data-capture invalidates entries built from that source. Proof: `test_policy_change_invalidates_cache_and_new_answer_served`.
25. **When is vector search the wrong tool?** Counting and exact lookups need structured queries. Proof: `test_vector_alone_is_wrong_for_counting`.
26. **How do you stop invented numbers?** A grounding check rejects figures not in the sources. Proof: `test_grounding_blocks_invented_number`.
27. **How do you keep one applicant's documents out of another's answer?** Hard filter by application id at retrieval. Proof: [case 5](interview-cases/05-mortgage-qa.md).

## F. Safety, guardrails and risk

28. **What does one wrong answer cost, and what follows?** High cost means human gates on writes, eval gates and abstaining below a confidence level. Proof: [case 4](interview-cases/04-healthcare-intake.md).
29. **How do you handle prompt injection?** Block before any model call and treat retrieved text as data. Proof: `test_injection_blocked_before_any_model_call`.
30. **How is PII kept out of logs and caches?** Redact before tracing and caching. Proof: `test_pii_never_reaches_traces_or_cache`.
31. **Bank-specific guardrail?** No personalised financial advice; route to a licensed person. Proof: [case 1](interview-cases/01-bank-assistant.md#deep-dive).
32. **Read tools vs write tools in an agent?** Reads run freely; writes need approval or a confidence gate, plus idempotency. Proof: `test_idempotency_key_dedupes`, `test_chaos_kill_replica_mid_workflow_resumes_and_refunds_once`.
33. **When do you send to a human?** Sensitive, angry, or low-confidence cases. Proof: `test_hitl_intents_never_reach_ai`.

## G. Data and compliance

34. **Residency vs data in transit?** Residency: stored data stays in-country. Transit: it may not even pass through other countries, so inference must be in-region too. Proof: `test_residency_never_leaves_region`.
35. **Can cached answers leak between tenants?** Not if keys include tenant and permissions. Proof: `test_cache_hit_not_shared_across_tenants`.
36. **How do permissions work in enterprise search?** ACLs indexed and applied before ranking. Proof: [case 6](interview-cases/06-enterprise-search.md).

## H. Reliability and operations

37. **The provider is down. What happens?** Breaker opens, fallback tier serves, budget refunded if nothing worked. Proof: `test_breaker_full_cycle`, `test_all_providers_down_degrades_honestly_and_refunds_budget`.
38. **A dependency is slow, not failing.** A slow-call breaker trips on latency, not errors. Proof: `test_slow_breaker_trips_on_slow_successes`.
39. **Long jobs during a spike?** Queue with bulkheads so interactive work stays fast. Proof: `test_bulkheads_isolate_pools`.
40. **Why share rate-limit state in Redis?** Replicas behind a load balancer must agree. Proof: `test_limits_shared_across_replicas`.

## I. Verification and change

41. **How do you know it works?** The envelope math plus a domain golden set, not public benchmarks. Proof: `test_functional_eval`.
42. **The vendor updated the model.** Version model and prompt together, rerun the golden set, canary, roll back. Proof: `test_version_pin_rolls_back`, `test_canary_shadow_ab`.
43. **What breaks first at 10x?** Quota, spend and the latency budget, roughly linearly; the 10x rows in each case show it. Proof: `test_scale_10x_linear_and_cache_reduces_model_load`.
44. **Why is caching "the saver"?** Repeated questions skip retrieval and the model. Proof: `test_cache_saves_cost_without_wrong_answers`, [caching.md](caching.md).
